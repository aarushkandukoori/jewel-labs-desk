Compliance first

The EU deadline is
already here.

Since 1 January 2026, every polished natural diamond of 0.5 carats and up entering the EU needs a Due Diligence Statement on Diamond Origin. The US asks for its own CBP self-certification. Most houses still build this proof by hand. Jewel Labs Desk builds it from the papers you already have: one upload in, a different statement per destination out, audit trail kept. The house only signs.

How it works

Seven agents run the desk.
Each one does one job.

They start on their own as papers arrive, pass the work along in order, and stop to ask when something is unclear.

  1. 01

    Retrieval

    Pulls mail, chats and folders

    Checks the connected mailbox every 5 minutes, unpacks WhatsApp chat exports and picks up files from the folder you choose.

  2. 02

    Intake

    Reads every document

    Sorts each paper by type and reads it with a vision model, with OCR as a fallback. Every value keeps the spot on the page it came from.

  3. 03

    Records

    Traces every stone

    Puts each paper under the right shipment and follows every stone from the rough to the air waybill.

  4. 04

    Sanctions

    Screens every party

    Checks each name on the papers against the US Treasury OFAC SDN list, refreshed every day.

  5. 05

    Origin

    Checks origin against the rules

    The 0.5 ct line, natural or lab-grown, Kimberley Process countries, Russia anywhere in the chain, weights and totals, and what each destination asks for.

  6. 06

    Review

    Flags what is missing

    Turns anything uncertain into a one-line question for your team. Nothing uncertain reaches a pack.

  7. 07

    Paperwork

    Writes and cites

    Builds the origin pack: the EU statement draft or the US self-certification, the stone list, the evidence index and the originals in the order customs reads them.

  8. You

    You sign. You send.

    A person has the last word

    Someone at the house reads the pack, signs it and sends it from the house's own mailbox. The signed pack is locked with a hash.

Security and privacy

Your papers stay
your papers.

Plain rules, built into the app and the server.

  • Keys never ship in the app

    There is no API key inside the download. The app signs in to your house's workspace instead.

  • Model calls go through our server

    The agents reach the model through the Jewel Labs server, which holds the key and checks who is asking.

  • Not used for training

    OpenAI's API does not train on API data by default.

  • One workspace per house

    Each house's shipments, papers and stones live in its own workspace. Members only see the houses they belong to.

  • Row-level security

    The database itself checks every read and write against the signed-in member, not only the app.

  • Every step logged

    Each agent step and each person's action lands in the activity log, with the time.

  • Nothing goes out unsigned

    A person signs every pack before it is sent. The signed pack is locked with a SHA-256 hash.

  • Passwords stay on the computer

    Mailbox app passwords are kept in the system's secure storage, never in plain files.

Questions

Good to know
before you install.

What does Jewel Labs Desk do?

It turns the papers for a diamond shipment into the pack your buyer and customs need. The agents read every document, match each stone across the papers, check origin and sanctions, and ask you about anything they are unsure of. You sign and send.

Does Jewel Labs lodge the statement for us?

No. The house signs the statement, and the house or its customs broker lodges it. Jewel Labs prepares the papers and can review them before they go out.

Which papers can the agents read?

Commercial invoices, packing lists, grading reports, Kimberley Process certificates, shipping bills, air waybills, insurance certificates, email threads, WhatsApp exports and phone photos. PDFs, scans and photos all work.

What happens when an agent is not sure?

It asks your team a one-line question, such as a missing report number or a weight that does not add up. Nothing uncertain goes into a pack.

Do we have to change how we work?

No. Keep sending papers the way you do now: email, WhatsApp or a shared folder. Connect the mailbox once and the agents pick up new shipment mail every 5 minutes.
